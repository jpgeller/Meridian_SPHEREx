Meridian with eye tracking

Control Meridian by looking at it. This needs your camera.

  • Camera frames go only to the team's eye-tracking engine, are used once to find where you look, and are never stored.
  • Nothing is recorded on this website's server.
  • Start with a calibration (about 4 minutes) for much better precision.
  • Best in Chrome or Edge, full screen, with your face evenly lit.
